Emily Neil

Bilingual multimedia journalist

About

I report on the suburban counties north and west of Philadelphia: local government and immigration enforcement, alongside the community and culture stories that come with them. I work in radio and web, and shoot my own photos.

Since 2020 I've co-hosted El Café de la Tarde, a weekly Spanish-language show on Philatinos Radio in South Philadelphia. Born and raised in Baltimore, based in Philadelphia.

My work has appeared in WHYY News, Billy Penn, The New York Times, The Philadelphia Citizen, The Trace, Religion News Service, Blind Magazine, Generocity, GridPhilly, IJNET and ¡Presente! Media.

I was a 2022–23 Religion News Service and Interfaith America fellow, and I've edited for Global Press Journal alongside reporters based around the world.
